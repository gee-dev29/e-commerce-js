# Research Software Engineer - Health Informatics

## Purpose of Role

The Health Informatics team in Lifespan and Population Health at the School of Medicine are leaders in national infrastructures to help make data from the National Health Service **Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable**. They lead the informatics programme in the NIHR Biomedical Research Centre and strategically assist Nottingham University Hospitals NHS Trust to understand the power of data for research.

You will be a Research Software Engineer, part of a wider team and working flexibly to deliver an efficient and effective software. You will work with independence and initiative to manage processes and projects and react to changing priorities and work circumstances, organising your work to meet your set objectives.

### Key Programmes
Our leading programmes that are likely to be included in this programme are:
- NIHR Nottingham Biomedical Research Centre
- NHS England Secure Data Environment Programme
- HDR Federated Analytics Programme
- DAREUK Trevolution
- Working on our world leading software products such as **Carrot, Hutch, Bunny, Lettuce**, that have been recognised by the Medical Research Council in impact awards

### Additional Information
Other reasonable duties commensurate with the grade, spirit and purpose of the post may be requested. You will be expected to work flexibly to support the Health Informatics community across the School's geographical sites.

The School of Medicine recognises the importance of continuous professional development and therefore, the importance of providing opportunities, structured support and encouragement to engage in professional development each year.

To find out more about the School of Medicine, its values, vision, teaching and research, please see our further information leaflet.

## Main Responsibilities

### 1. Delivering Software (70% of time per year)
- With support from senior team members design, develop, implement, and document research-focused software projects
- Responsible for the development of software requiring specialist skills, techniques and knowledge and interacting with the users to capture requirements
- Generate original ideas and innovative solutions through the provision of specialist know-how and advice as appropriate
- Where the software brings new knowledge, contribute to research outputs such as:
  - Research publications
  - Impact case studies
  - Technical advice in support of grant applications

### 2. Management and Researcher Engagement (20% of time per year)
- Ensuring work is carried out and performed to the required standard, taking the necessary follow-up measures to ensure effective performance
- Undertake critical review at key stages of the project to understand if processes and delivery methods can be improved

### 3. Wider Support (10% of time per year)
- Acts as a recognised source of technical and/or experimental expertise in own area of technical skill/expertise through the effective utilisation and application of substantial skill and experience
- Acts as an internal and external consultant, advising on the development and application of specialist software techniques in research

### 4. Other Duties
- Any other duties appropriate to the grade and level of the role
